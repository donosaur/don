About

The person behind the work.

Hi 👋 I'm Don. I'm a Brooklyn-based product designer who has spent 15+ years in streaming video and consumer electronics. I started designing 10-foot TV interfaces at Boxee, helped integrate our software into Samsung Smart TVs after acquisition, then built Vimeo's OTT creator platform across 11 platforms including web, mobile, and every major TV OS. Along the way, I've designed for holographic displays at Looking Glass and earned an AI for UX Design certification. I use AI tools throughout my design process — from rapid concept exploration with generative AI to prototyping with AI-assisted tools.

Career narrative

How I got here.

My design journey started with small hardware startups and led to larger streaming platforms. I've learned a lot at every step. Even through two acquisitions, I chose to stay with the product to ensure our work continued to serve the people using it.

July 2010 – July 2013
Boxee
Designer · New York, NY

Where it all started. At Boxee, I helped design early Cloud DVR and social TV features when the streaming space was still brand new. I also pitched in on packaging and marketing for our hardware. In 2013, Samsung acquired Boxee, which was a great next step for the product we had built together.

Cloud DVR Social TV 0→1 Hardware launch Acquired by Samsung
July 2013 – Nov 2015
Samsung
Senior Product Designer · New York, NY

Following Boxee's acquisition by Samsung, I designed multi-screen experiences for Samsung's Smart TV ecosystem — integrating Boxee software into Samsung's TV interface and contributing to the design of next-generation remotes. This chapter taught me how to scale consumer-grade design thinking inside a massive hardware organization.

Smart TV Multi-screen Hardware integration 10-foot UI
