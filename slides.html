CASE STUDY 01

Vimeo OTT: Scaling the Screen

11 Platforms Unified UI Architecture Whitelabel Systems

The Challenge: "One update, 11 Platforms"

Vimeo OTT needed a way to let creators launch custom apps without our engineering team building a unique app every single time.

  • ✕
    Massive fragmentation across Tizen, Roku, tvOS, FireTV, and Web.
  • ✕
    Unsustainable maintenance cost for "one-off" customer features.
  • ✕
    Lack of a centralized "Source of Truth" for global styling.
TV

Multi-Platform Ecosystem

The Solution: Component DNA

Developing a design system based on "Flexible Foundations" that adapted to hardware constraints.

01

Tokenized Design

Moved all styling to a central JSON engine that pushes updates to all 11 SDKs simultaneously.

02

Focus Logic

Unified remote-control navigation (D-pad) behaviors across different OS hardware.

03

Self-Serve CMS

Enabled creators to live-preview their whitelabel brand on all devices before publishing.
